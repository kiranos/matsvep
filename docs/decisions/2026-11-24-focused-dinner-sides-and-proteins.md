# Focused dinner sides and protein evidence

Production samples after the positive dinner evidence pass still contained standalone sauces, pickles, drinks, desserts and protein-free starch dishes. Some wrapped fish dishes selected the wrapping meat, while stock cubes and oyster sauce produced false protein matches.

- Add the preparation and side terms as seed data. Leading dish terms use the existing title boundary and protein or dinner-word exemption. An unambiguous dessert title still excludes.
- A potato, rice, couscous, bulgur or salad title without a substantial meat, fish or vegetarian protein item is a side, including when it contains a dinner dish word or has a main-dish source tag. An explicit non-dinner source tag still excludes, and manual overrides remain final. Untagged sill titles derive buffet and are excluded.
- When the leading dish name contains a specific fish or meat and a seeded wrapping or flavour meat, select the other specific protein. Keep the title's original first-protein order when the other protein is only in an accompaniment.
- Recognise stock cubes and powders as stock evidence, which can rule out vegetarian but cannot set main protein. Animal fat used only for flavour has the same protein-selection limit. Keep oyster sauce and oyster mushrooms out of the oyster stem through seed exceptions.
- Rerun derivation on the pilot database and review candidate and protein changes before any production processing.
